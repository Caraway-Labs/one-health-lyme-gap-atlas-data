The VIIRS Collection 2 science team reports cross-calibration of *surface reflectance* across VIIRS platforms and MODIS Aqua. That statement does not establish harmonized MOD13Q1 Terra and VIIRS **vegetation-index outputs**, QA, or county time series. No cross-sensor or cross-platform splice is authorized. NASA positions VNP/VJ products as successors in mission continuity, not as an Atlas-equivalent measure without validation.

### Publisher metadata and unresolved continuity

All four active products are distributed through NASA CMR discovery and LP DAAC Earthdata Cloud with Earthdata-authenticated HTTPS data access. NASA's open-data terms permit use with attribution; credentialed transport does not imply a restricted scientific license. MOD13Q1.061 is the Terra MODIS 250 m / 16-day sinusoidal HDF4 product. VNP13A1.002, VJ113A1.002, and VJ213A1.002 are Suomi NPP, NOAA-20, and NOAA-21 VIIRS 500 m / 16-day sinusoidal HDF-EOS5 products respectively. Their start dates and active CMR identities are in the table. The CMR records for all three VIIRS collections link the same LP DAAC VIIRS V2 guide. It specifies NDVI/EVI/EVI2 signed values at a `0.0001` multiplier and distinct native water/land fill codes (`-15000`/`-13000`); those codes must not be interpreted with MODIS's `-3000` fill rule. VIIRS QA fill is `65535`; its reliability fill is `-1` over land or `-4` over water/ocean. A source-backed, platform-specific VJ13 SDS/QA/fill verification remains prerequisite to any VJ implementation, so none is selected here.

The linked VIIRS V2 guide's tiled-product VI Quality uses bits 0–1 for MODLAND production quality, 2–5 for VI usefulness, 6–7 for aerosol, 8 for adjacent cloud, 9 for BRDF correction, 10 for mixed clouds, 11–13 for land/water, 14 for snow/ice, and 15 for possible shadow. The VIIRS land/water legend identifies `000` as land/desert and `001` as land/no desert, unlike the selected MODIS v1 rule that admits only `001`. VIIRS pixel reliability ranks `0` excellent, `1` good, `2` acceptable, `3` marginal, `7` cloud shadow, `8` snow/ice, and `9` cloud. These are publisher guide semantics for the family, not a validated rule for VJ county extraction. The bit layout's similarity to MODIS does not make the radiometric series or source-supported-area masks interchangeable.

The MOD13Q1 VI Quality legend describes cloud through MODLAND 10, adjacent-cloud bit 8, mixed-cloud bit 10, and pixel reliability 3; snow/ice through bit 14 and pixel reliability 2. Fill is VI `-3000`, QA `65535`, reliability/DOY `-1`. The product is a best-pixel composite, not a daily observation. The date range identifies the composite; selected pixels have their own day-of-year values.

Publisher CMR update, HDF production time, and HTTP Last-Modified are retained separately. These timestamps can change under collection reprocessing, regenerated granules, or metadata revisions. The one observed 2025 composite was produced/updated roughly four days after its period ended, but this is **one sample**, not a publication latency guarantee. No product here has a verified Atlas point-in-time backfill history or an approved late-publication SLA. Future candidates require a separate revision/availability study before time-series admission. For MOD13Q1 v1, any recapture is immutable and the conservative availability date is the retained capture time.

## Frozen v1 measure and policy decision

Only `MOD13Q1.061` is selected. The v1 outputs are two **Atlas-derived**, county-by-16-day-composite, unitless measures: `MODIS_NDVI_MEAN` and `MODIS_EVI_MEAN`. Each is the #424 exact-intersection-area weighted mean of the publisher's scaled index for pixels passing the same strict QA mask. The denominator is QA-valid, source-supported land area within the full frozen 2025 TIGER county polygon. These are observed vegetation-condition summaries; neither is a tick-abundance, habitat-risk, or disease-risk score. They are not automatically approved ML features.

`MOD13Q1.061` identifies the Terra source only. Retain the exact 16-day period, each selected pixel's composite DOY range/distribution, source production timestamp, CMR update timestamp, object Last-Modified, retrieval timestamp, collection, granule identity, and content SHA-256 separately. Original historical availability is **not** inferred from the observation period or filename. For point-in-time use, the captured artifact is conservatively known available no earlier than its first retained retrieval time unless a separately reviewed publication record proves earlier availability. Reprocessed historical granules become new #426 immutable captures/revisions; they never overwrite a prior capture.

For each pixel, accept only native VI Quality MODLAND bits 0-1 = `00`, pixel reliability = `0`, land/water bits 11-13 = `001` (unambiguous land), no adjacent or mixed cloud (bits 8/10), no possible snow/ice (bit 14), no possible shadow (bit 15), VI usefulness at most `0010`, and NDVI/EVI raw values within `-2000..10000` excluding `-3000`. Marginal, cloud, snow/ice, shadow, water/coast, QA fill, and VI fill remain separate area categories in the source-derived evidence even though all are excluded from either mean. A valid raw zero is numerical zero. No masked value is zero-filled. The common mask ensures NDVI and EVI share a denominator. Native QA bit values and counts are retained in the normalized record for audit.

The source-supported footprint is the area of HDF cells with a non-fill QA land flag `001` (publisher legend: "Land (Nothing else but land)"), distinct from (1) full legal county area, (2) raster-tile intersection area, and (3) strict QA-valid area. Their fractions are recorded separately. **Completeness model after the bounded threshold study:** a full county/tile intersection with positive source-supported and positive QA-valid land area yields the strict-mask mean. `COMPLETE` means every supported land cell passed the strict mask; otherwise the numeric mean is `PARTIAL_COVERAGE` and remains explicitly a mean of the observed QA-valid subset, not a county-wide estimate. Zero QA-valid area, missing tile/composite, or zero support yields null; a partially intersected tile yields `SOURCE_MISSING` and null even if some pixels are valid. No numeric QA fraction is asserted as a NASA rule or a representativeness guarantee. The v1 definition remains limited to one explicitly named composite, tile, and county; no historical or CONUS crawl is implied.

Phenology onset, peak, senescence, seasonal extrema, and anomalies are **DEFERRED**. A single 16-day composite cannot support a seasonal curve, and #381 has not approved a smoothing, season, reference period, or gap model. No timing is inferred from the composite DOY field alone.

## Bounded source evidence and operational limits

The CMR record for `MOD13Q1.A2025193.h09v05.061.2025212122804` identifies a 2025-07-12 through 2025-07-27 composite and CMR update `2025-07-31T11:33:10.874Z`. Its HTTPS object returned 265,246,608 bytes, HDF4 signature `0E 03 13 01`, and SHA-256 `ed7b25030470e711a9cd4f6155361844ed7720f5da44bdf5fa1b71822c3b0935` on 2026-09-26. A bounded authenticated range GET returned 206, `binary/octet-stream`, 16 bytes, and the same HDF4 signature. The full file yielded twelve publisher SDSs; NDVI/EVI, VI Quality, reliability, and composite DOY had the expected 4,800 × 4,800 shape and NASA documented fills, valid ranges, and scale. The paired #424 TIGER 2025 ZIP returned 83,989,800 bytes and its approved SHA-256 `9c6e9d9076abce2670d1de255de3710c35ecca00a7005d88e012dec52d95f763`.

The two checked artifacts total 349,236,408 bytes. The first SourceDefinition caps the HDF member at 300,000,000 bytes, TIGER at 100,000,000 bytes, and the full run at 384,000,000 bytes. These caps include measured headroom without authorizing a second tile. A larger future granule requires a reviewed definition/bound change; it is never silently accepted. The CMR production and update times are evidence for this one granule, **not** a latency service level or proof of first historical availability. The object Last-Modified was `Thu, 31 Jul 2025 21:41:24 GMT`. Snapshot staleness is therefore distinct from acquisition failure.

The implemented adapter's live bounded acquisition also completed through `requests.Session()` using the existing local Earthdata credential chain. It returned exactly three members (HDF, CMR JSON, TIGER ZIP), with the HDF at 265,246,608 bytes and `binary/octet-stream`; the HDF and TIGER SHA-256 values matched the retained source-backed references. CMR's transport used gzip, so its wire `Content-Length` is not compared to its decoded JSON byte count. This is HTTP acquisition evidence, not a governed DEV warehouse run.

The Texas county `48081` exploratory window in the verified HDF was rows `3797:3989`, columns `2021:2436`. It contained 45,441 intersecting raster cells by an all-touched screening mask; 43,468 carried the land QA flag and 43,443 passed the strict mask. Screening means were NDVI `0.48359` and EVI `0.29648`; these are **not** final exact-area county outputs. Good/reliability-good observations dominated; snow and cloud did not occur in this one window. Fixture tests exercise those QA states. The tile contains 24 marginal pixels and 17 NDVI values outside its valid range; no value was coerced to zero.

The completed #424 exact-boundary-area run on these retained bytes yielded county area `2,403,485,190.62 m²`, intersected tile area `2,403,485,190.61 m²`, source-supported land area `2,299,790,322.66 m²`, and common strict QA-valid area `2,298,449,720.98 m²`. The QA-valid fraction of supported land was `0.9994170766`. The weighted NDVI was `0.4838826701`; weighted EVI was `0.2966664927`. Valid composite DOYs ranged from 194 to 206. QA category areas are independent flags and may overlap; they are not an additive partition.

**Decision: CHANGE MODEL.** The old 0.99 numeric publication gate would suppress substantial QA-valid observations in both a humid eastern summer composite and a northern autumn composite; lowering it to fit four examples would be an unsupported new cutoff. The v1 value is explicitly the mean of the strict QA-valid subset, with its exact fraction and `PARTIAL_COVERAGE` status retained for any QA loss. This is a candidate internal semantic measure, with no DEV, PROD, API, score, or ML admission. This small purposive sample is not nationally representative, does not establish a safe minimum fraction for downstream inference, and includes no observed snow case; snow remains publisher-legend and fixture-backed. The high water/coast fractions for `27053` reflect this conservative native `001` definition and are not silently reclassified as supported land. Later publication or model use needs separate stewardship review of source-support and QA fractions.
