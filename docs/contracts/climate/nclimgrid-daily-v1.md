## Source and artifact policy

SELECT NOAA NCEI **nClimGrid-Daily v1.0.0**, monthly **scaled** NetCDF daily grids at `https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/data/nclimgrid-daily/access/grids/{YYYY}/ncdd-{YYYYMM}-grd-scaled.nc`. The January 2025 file responded HTTP 200 on 2026-09-25, with 61,013,299 bytes, `Last-Modified: Sat, 05 Apr 2025 04:02:03 GMT`, and SHA-256 `809a58714578ce654e61e094e5f7d0ee704d332f1ff6a86c644e56de4ee4da31`. NOAA's product page identifies v1.0.0 and CONUS coverage; its user guide distinguishes `scaled` (aligned to monthly nClimGrid/nClimDdiv) from `prelim` (not yet scaled). A governed monthly source definition pins one scaled month. It never silently substitutes a preliminary file. New scaled bytes at the same URL require a new run/capture and physical revision; prior bytes remain immutable. Preliminary availability is operationally distinct and is not admitted by this v1 definition.

The run retains two named, separately SHA-256-checked source-faithful members: `noaa-scaled-monthly-netcdf` (`SOURCE_PAYLOAD`) and `tiger-2025-analysis-county-zip` (`ANALYSIS_REFERENCE`). The latter must match #424's exact 2025 TIGER archive for live runs. VALIDATE and NORMALIZE load both from the #432 run manifest; they never refetch after ACQUIRE. The frozen canonical FIPS comparison fixture is packaged with the runtime wheel and tested byte-for-byte against #424's governed contract fixture. V103 partitions and capture revisions from #426 remain the physical record ledger. Stable normalized rows include both member names/digests, 2025 geometry digest and transform version, grid and weight IDs, source variable, observation date, NOAA file modification metadata, and transformation version. The run-specific member artifact IDs, actual retrieval time, and HTTP modification header stay in the ACQUIRE checkpoint and V103 capture lineage so identical recaptures retain the same physical content revision.

## Grid and measure interpretation

The checked NOAA January 2025 file has `time=31`, `lat=596`, `lon=1385`, `prcp`, `tmin`, `tmax`, and `tavg` in that dimension order. `time` is Gregorian whole days since `1800-01-01 00:00:00`; its dates are 2025-01-01 through 2025-01-31. `lat` and `lon` are ascending one-dimensional grid-point coordinates in `degrees_north` and `degrees_east` at nominal 1/24° spacing. The coordinates are treated as cell centroids, with deterministic edges halfway between adjacent coordinates and half-step exterior edges; the footprint CRS is EPSG:4326. The #424 algorithm projects TIGER counties and native cell footprints to EPSG:5070 for CONUS area intersection. The monthly grid identity hashes the ordered coordinate arrays and CRS. Per-county reusable weights are built once per run and keyed by grid identity, county geometry digest, TIGER artifact SHA-256, and #424 weight method version; daily variables reuse those weights.

The four county-day measures are `PRCP` (native `prcp`, precipitation in mm), `TMIN` (`tmin`, °C), `TMAX` (`tmax`, °C), and NOAA-supplied `TAVG` (`tavg`, °C). Each is the valid-area-weighted mean of intersecting grid cells. TAVG is read from NOAA; it is never recalculated from TMIN/TMAX. NOAA's `_FillValue` is NaN in the checked artifact. Its numeric PRCP field does not encode a distinct trace state; `0.00` is retained as numeric zero, never relabeled trace. Missing or nonfinite cells contribute no valid area and never become zero. The source time denotes a 24-hour period ending in the early morning of the labeled date, as stated in NOAA variable metadata. Observation date is not a publication or availability timestamp.

## County coverage and completeness

The product is CONUS-only. Every canonical Alaska/Hawaii county receives a null value with `OUT_OF_SOURCE_COVERAGE`, not an expected-missing row, substitute, or zero. For eligible counties, `expected_area_m2` is the full projected 2025 TIGER polygon and `intersected_area_m2` is the intersection with the rectangular grid-cell footprints under #424. Those geometric footprints include NOAA fill-valued water cells and must not be treated as the supported climate-data area. The shared monthly source-support mask is the union of finite, non-fill cells across all available days and all four variables in the retained scaled NetCDF. `source_supported_area_m2` is the weighted intersection with that mask; `source_coverage_fraction` is supported / expected. This explicitly records source spatial support over the legal county, without claiming it equals a Census land polygon. A cell missing in all four variables for the entire month is indistinguishable from the product mask in this file alone; that limitation remains visible as reduced source coverage.

Daily completeness is separate: `valid_fraction_of_supported_area` is the measure/day's finite, non-fill `valid_area_m2` divided by `source_supported_area_m2`. The v1 **95% engineering threshold applies to this daily fraction**, not to full legal-county area or the rectangular grid intersection. `COMPLETE` emits the valid-area-weighted value (including legitimate zero PRCP) when this fraction is at least 0.95. `PARTIAL_COVERAGE` retains null for positive but insufficient daily valid area. `SOURCE_MISSING` retains null when no source-supported cells have a valid daily value or the monthly support area is zero. A day absent from the monthly time axis is emitted for every canonical county with `source_time_present=false` and the same missing/out-of-source distinction. Source support may be far below 95% of legal county area even when daily data are complete; both fractions and the three area values must remain available to reviewers and downstream mappings. The 95% policy is Atlas-specific, not a NOAA scientific constant or a claim that a small supported footprint represents the entire legal county.

The January 2025 source-backed review of the exact NOAA SHA-256 and #424 TIGER SHA-256 found 3,109 eligible CONUS counties. Geometric footprint/full-polygon intersection was at least 95% for 3,107 counties (minimum 93.75%). The NOAA finite-value mask was identical across all four measures and 31 days, yet only 2,795 counties had at least 95% source-supported/full-polygon area. The former full-polygon threshold suppressed 9,734 PRCP county-days with complete data within the January source-supported footprint. The reviewed mask is a reproducible property of the retained monthly artifact, not an independently supplied NOAA land-boundary polygon. Census `ALAND`/`AWATER` attributes corroborate that legal polygons include water, but `ALAND` is an area attribute rather than a spatial mask for intersecting individual cells.

NOAA describes its own published region averages as cosine-latitude-weighted values at grid points **within** a region, with nearest-grid-point handling for tracts smaller than a grid box. It describes land coverage but does not specify a separate coastal-water denominator or a county water-mask rule. Atlas continues to aggregate the scaled grid with its reviewed #424 projected cell-intersection weights; it does not import NOAA county CSVs or claim numerical identity with NOAA's published county averages.

The stable logical physical record identity is source definition + canonical county FIPS + observation date + measure. A byte-identical recapture keeps the same source content and physical revision identity; a changed NOAA artifact creates a new immutable capture even when the county value is unchanged. #426's run-pinned reader distinguishes those captures. Historical publication time is not available from the checked old file; preserve actual retrieval, HTTP `Last-Modified` when supplied, NetCDF `date_modified`, and NOAA product/version, and mark original publication time unavailable for later point-in-time ML review. These metadata dates are not inferred from the observation day.

## #202 compatibility and operator matrix

| Dimension | Required nClimGrid fact and check | Incompatible or action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product/vintage | v1.0.0 scaled, exact configured YYYYMM | Preliminary, changed version, or wrong month blocks normalization. |
| Geography | CONUS 2025 TIGER canonical FIPS; grid EPSG:4326, area EPSG:5070 | AK/HI are out of source coverage; different county vintage or display geometry needs review. |
| Time | County-day labeled 24-hour observation period | Annual/monthly or point-in-time joins need an approved transformation; no automatic aggregation. |
| Unit | PRCP mm; temperatures °C; no denominator | Changed unit or source variable fails validation and #195 meaning comparison. |
| Completeness | Source coverage = monthly supported intersection / full TIGER polygon; daily completeness = valid / supported, with 95% applied only to the latter | Preserve both fractions and geometric intersection; partial/missing stay null, never zero-fill or hide unsupported area. |
| Freshness/availability | Monthly scaled publication; HTTP/NetCDF modified and retrieval captured separately | A quiet period is not a failed refresh by itself. A failed scheduled retrieval is operationally distinct. Historic first availability is unavailable. |
| Revision | Content SHA-256 and V103 run-specific immutable capture | Changed bytes require new capture; previously approved release is not replaced automatically. |
| Lineage | NOAA + TIGER members, grid/weight/geometry identities, transformation and run | Missing or corrupt member blocks replay; no worker-local fallback. |
| Scope | Contextual evidence only | No causal Lyme-risk, score, PRISM duplicate, rolling feature, or ML approval. |

The four candidate #190 `COUNTY` / one-day `PERIOD` derived measure definitions are in `nclimgrid-semantic-measures-v1.json`; the #192 mapping registry binds each to its native variable and checks the NOAA/TIGER lineage pair, coverage state, county, unit, and date before constructing a semantic assertion. The source definition and physical rows do not themselves create #191 reviewed metadata, #193 approved lineage authority, a #195 compatible release, or #194 consumer exposure. Source approval and a reviewed semantic release remain separate gates. No DEV or PROD persistence is implied by fixture tests.
