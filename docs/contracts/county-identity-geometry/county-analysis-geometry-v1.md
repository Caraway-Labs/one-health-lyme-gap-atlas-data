# County analysis geometry and grid weighting v1

Status: proposed for human review under Story #424 and [ADR 0036](../../adr/0036-county-analysis-geometry-and-area-weighting.md). Owner: Atlas data stewardship and engineering. This contract is additive to [county identity and display geometry v1](county-identity-geometry-v1.md), not a change to its 3,144 identities, existing SVI geometry, county release, API, or public map. The implementation is `county_analysis_geometry.py` and fixture tests are `test_county_analysis_geometry.py`.

## Source decision and scope

SELECT the U.S. Census Bureau **2025 TIGER/Line County and Equivalent Entity National Shapefile (Current)**, archive `https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/TIGER2025/COUNTY/tl_2025_us_county.zip`. Legal boundaries and names are as of **January 1, 2025**; the release was September 23, 2025. The exact locally evaluated archive is 83,989,800 bytes with SHA-256 `9c6e9d9076abce2670d1de255de3710c35ecca00a7005d88e012dec52d95f763`. A later publisher revision at the same URL needs a new reviewed artifact/version and reconciliation. [Census release](https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/2025/geo/tiger-line-file.html); [2025 technical documentation](https://www2.census.gov/geo/pdfs/maps-data/data/tiger/tgrshp2025/TGRSHP2025_TechDoc.pdf).

The native `.prj` is NAD83 geographic (EPSG:4269). `STATEFP` (two digits), `COUNTYFP` (three digits), `GEOID` (their five-digit concatenation), and `MTFCC=G4020` identify counties and county equivalents; names are descriptive only. Census materials may be reproduced with source citation under the federal government work rule stated in Chapter 1 of its technical documentation. Census disclaims positional and attribute accuracy and says the boundaries are for statistical use, not legal land descriptions. This is the unsimplified TIGER/Line product; Census cartographic boundary products and the generalized SVI display layer are prohibited as inputs to weighting.

The national archive has 3,235 records. Atlas's frozen 50 states plus DC scope selects exactly 3,144 and excludes the 91 records in Puerto Rico and island areas (`STATEFP` 60, 66, 69, 72, 78). The source is expected to include 51 state/district codes, including Alaska and Hawaii. This scope is the existing Atlas county release scope, not a new judgment on the scientific suitability of a future source's coverage.

## Exact identity reconciliation

The committed `canonical-county-fips-2022.txt` is the sorted 3,144-ID comparison fixture extracted from the frozen `alpha-2026-08-06` local bundle (bundle SHA-256 `dd7408c7ce0f8c55623c3319cec813c0e28f3b0c81929197b1958f2a4a47be7c`), whose ID set is the governed SVI semantic-release identity. The 2025 TIGER archive's selected GEOID set equals that fixture exactly: **canonical-only 0; TIGER-only 0; duplicate GEOID 0**. Runtime validation requires the caller to supply the current canonical FIPS set independently and rejects any difference before output. A changed canonical set is outside #424 and requires review.

Edge-case inspection: Connecticut has the same nine planning-region GEOIDs `09110` through `09190`; SVI labels include “Planning Region” while TIGER `NAME` omits that suffix, so names are never join keys. Alaska has 30 borough/census-area equivalents, including multipart islands. County-independent cities are retained by GEOID, including Virginia and the corresponding Maryland, Missouri and Nevada entities. Renames and reorganizations with unchanged GEOID do not alter identity; any future split, merger, retired ID, or newly introduced ID blocks this contract's exact-set validator. No county is fabricated, remapped by name, or silently dropped.

## Geometry representation and lineage

Two geometry concepts remain distinct:

| Purpose | Source | Permitted use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display | CDC/ATSDR SVI 2022 FeatureServer, `maxAllowableOffset=0.01`, EPSG:4326 | Existing map and fixed semantic-release `county_geometry` / physical `geometry` observation. |
| Analysis | Pinned 2025 Census TIGER/Line county shapefile, unsimplified, EPSG:4269 | Raster/grid intersection and area weighting under this contract only. |

The ZIP bytes must be retained as an immutable source artifact by the existing governed artifact mechanism before downstream use. Caller supplies its actual artifact SHA-256; the loader verifies it and records product, vintage, artifact digest, source GEOID, a digest of original shapefile geometry coordinates, normalized OGC geometry digest, source/storage CRS, and transform version `atlas-county-analysis-geometry/1`. The source coordinate sequence is converted to OGC Polygon/MultiPolygon without simplification, vertex filtering, snapping, buffering, make-valid, or other repair. All parts, holes, and islands are retained. The original ZIP remains the source-faithful evidence; the coordinate digest and normalized digest make transformation repeatable. A future persisted analytical result must carry the geometry version/artifact digest alongside its existing #188 source artifact, run, transformation, and semantic lineage anchors. This contract creates no competing semantic measure, lineage table, or public field.

Every selected geometry must be nonempty, valid, polygonal, and positive area. Invalid source geometry is a blocking error; any needed deterministic repair requires a separate reviewed method, test, and retained original. Reprojection must yield valid geometry; no automatic repair occurs. Storage stays in source EPSG:4269. Area operations use `always_xy=True` projection to fixed equal-area CRSs: EPSG:5070 for states/DC except Alaska and Hawaii, EPSG:3338 for Alaska, and EPSG:2782 for Hawaii. This avoids using longitude/latitude degrees as area. Projected area and intersections are in square metres. Pin the artifact, transformation version, library lockfile, CRS definitions, grid geometry/CRS, cell IDs, and calculation version for replay.

## Grid-to-county mean primitive

The primitive accepts native cell footprints and values, their declared CRS and unit, plus a caller-selected **minimum valid-area completeness** in `[0,1]`. This contract does not invent a universal threshold for all environmental sources; each consumer must document its threshold and nodata code before use. Cells must be unique and nonoverlapping. Cell footprints and county geometry are projected into the county's equal-area CRS; intersection area is the weight. A cell that touches only a boundary has zero area and contributes no weight. Multipart components and holes participate exactly as polygon intersection defines them. No center-point inclusion, rasterization, or edge-cell rounding is used.

`intersected_area_m2` is the summed area of all intersecting cells (including nodata). `valid_area_m2` sums only cells with finite values. `completeness = valid_area_m2 / county_area_m2`, capped at 1 only for numerical roundoff. A finite value is reported as `sum(value * valid intersection area) / valid_area_m2` when valid area exists and completeness meets the caller's threshold. The input unit is preserved. `None` is nodata, never zero. No intersections yield `NO_INTERSECTING_CELLS`; all nodata yield `NO_VALID_CELLS`; below-threshold valid coverage yields `INCOMPLETE` with null value. The result carries county FIPS, unit, area diagnostics, completeness, status, artifact digest, geometry version, and weighting version. A source-specific story must interpret incomplete days/windows separately rather than fill them.

Cells are sorted by stable cell ID and summed with `math.fsum`; duplicate IDs, overlapping footprints, nonfinite values, invalid geometry, and intersection area exceeding county area by more than relative `1e-8` fail. Overlap tolerance is relative `1e-10` with a square-metre floor. Threshold comparison allows `1e-12` floating-point tolerance. Identical pinned inputs and library environment must repeat the same result; cross-library bit identity is not asserted. The primitive is a continuous-area polygon method. A source grid using curvilinear cells or changing datum must supply truthful cell footprints and CRS and revalidate area behavior before acceptance.

## Historical panel policy and limits

The first environmental panel uses **one frozen 2025 analysis county vintage** for every observation period, including historical climate decades, to support reproducible longitudinal comparison. A value for 1951 is an aggregation over the frozen 2025 polygon, **not** a reconstruction of the county's legal 1951 boundary. This limitation must accompany downstream metadata and analysis output. Historical administrative-boundary reconstruction or another geometry vintage requires a separate reviewed contract and new method/version. #424 does not ingest nClimGrid (#198), NLCD, MODIS, EnviroAtlas, or 3DEP; perform a DEV/PROD load; or change the current display/semantic release.

## Evidence and release gate

This is **local source-backed verification**, not a governed artifact registration, source approval, DEV load, PROD publication, or consumer proof. Those steps require their existing governed controls and separate evidence when a source story consumes this primitive.
