# Surveillance coverage and evidence gaps v1

Status: Proposed methodology for Story #170; implementation, publication, and formal steward approval remain separate
Owner: Atlas data stewardship and engineering
Methodology version: `surveillance-coverage-v1`
Companion eligibility matrix: `surveillance-coverage-v1-source-eligibility.json`

## Meaning and source boundary

Coverage here means **the presence of specified surveillance evidence at its source-native geography and time**, not biological presence, absence, sampling adequacy, reporting success, or risk. An evidence gap means the required evidence for one named construct is missing under this method. It does not establish underreporting, neglect, a surveillance failure, or a need to allocate resources. The CDC county-status workbooks and NSF NEON site/event records are separate evidence families and have no common coverage score.

Eligible CDC inputs are the governed Ixodes vector county-status and separately governed *B. burgdorferi* pathogen-status source versions. Eligible active inputs are only `DP1.10093.001` collection and `DP1.10092.001` individual pathogen testing in frozen `RELEASE-2026`, with the governed `tick-surveillance-v1.2` and normalization registry `1.0.4`. A source version must be approved and pinned; a missing, unauthorized, or unavailable version yields `UNAVAILABLE`. The bounded NEON DEV proof (`BLAN`, `2016-05`) is not a general site or public release.

Every result is a **derived categorical profile**, with method version, exact source/version/vintage, native record and canonical input IDs where present, run/artifact/retrieval references in the internal envelope, transformation version, quality profile and propagated reasons, and limitations. Consumer-safe projection excludes restricted artifact paths and raw content. Source-reported status remains a distinct input. No result is a numeric score.

For all constructs, a provenance or canonical structural failure prevents a positive state and is `UNAVAILABLE` with a reason. `UNKNOWN` records a missing or ambiguous fact without converting it to zero. State names are construct-specific; neither `PUBLISHER_NO_RECORDS` nor `NOT_REPORTED_IN_DATASET` means biological absence. `NOT_REPORTED_IN_DATASET` is asserted only from a complete and scoped snapshot, never from a partial query. County rows with an unresolvable FIPS are retained as source-only/unresolved evidence and do not increment canonical representation. A noncanonical geography outside the approved canonical universe is `NOT_APPLICABLE` to the county construct. Duplicate identical rows count once; conflicting or unreconciled revisions yield `UNKNOWN`, retaining every candidate and revision. No automatic latest-row selection is authorized. A source flagged `STALE_OR_REVISION_SENSITIVE` retains its categorical state with a visible freshness limitation; no universal age cutoff is approved.

## Site, geography, and continuity

Site/event results retain source coordinates, CRS/uncertainty, plot and event identifiers, `UNMAPPED` or `AMBIGUOUS` county relationship, and `NOT_COUNTY_REPRESENTATIVE`. A mapped county may be shown as navigation context only. It never receives `COUNTY_COVERED`, county active-surveillance density, or site prevalence. Unmapped and ambiguous sites remain eligible for native event evidence if their site/event identity is sound; they cannot be assigned to a canonical county.

Repeated events remain distinct, ordered source-native observations. A single event establishes only event evidence. An inventory may list observed event dates at one site, with method and source version preserved. It cannot assert site-period completeness, missing scheduled events, a temporal gap, or continuity without an approved expected schedule/cadence and period scope. `TEMPORAL_CONTINUITY_AVAILABLE` and any site-period coverage percentage are deferred. Missing event rows are `UNKNOWN`, not proof of an unsampled site. `samplingImpractical` is not a completed zero sample. A documented completed zero-tick event is sampled evidence; a zero-positive individual test with positive tested denominator is testing evidence. Neither is biological absence outside its native stratum.

The current canonical NEON adapter emits collection observations from `tck_taxonomyProcessed` rows joined to `tck_fielddata`; a field event with no taxonomy row is not automatically represented as a canonical zero-tick observation. #171 may assert sampled-zero only when a retained canonical collection observation actually carries zero count and valid effort. An absent canonical row remains `UNKNOWN` unless a separately reviewed event-completeness input and mapping is added. This specification does not authorize that mapping or infer zero from the absence of rows.

## Quality, comparability, and interpretation

Use `surveillance-quality-profile-v1` on each canonical input and `surveillance-quality-propagation-v1` on a derived envelope. `TECHNICAL_SOURCE_VALIDITY` and `PROVENANCE_COMPLETENESS` must be `ASSESSED` for a positive state. The active sampling and effort states require `EFFORT_DENOMINATOR_COMPLETENESS` `ASSESSED` with `EFFORT_DOCUMENTED` and a checked positive effort; testing availability requires `PATHOGEN_TESTING_DENOMINATOR_VALIDITY` `ASSESSED` with `INDIVIDUAL_TEST_DENOMINATOR_VALID`. County status does not require active effort or a testing denominator: those components are `NOT_APPLICABLE`. `METHOD_DOCUMENTATION` is required for the active collection method; no method equivalence follows. Other `UNKNOWN` components are retained as unknown explanations rather than zero coverage; `NOT_APPLICABLE` is never promoted to evidence. Preserve `METHOD_COMPARABILITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED`, `PARTIAL_SPATIAL_COVERAGE`, `UNMAPPED_SITE_GEOGRAPHY`, `AMBIGUOUS_SITE_GEOGRAPHY`, `SITE_EVENT_NOT_COUNTY_REPRESENTATIVE`, `EFFORT_UNKNOWN`, `EFFORT_UNAVAILABLE`, `SAMPLING_IMPRACTICAL`, `TEST_DENOMINATOR_UNAVAILABLE`, `STALE_OR_REVISION_SENSITIVE`, and source limitations where present. Quality has no hidden numeric weight or confidence interpretation.

Compare only the same construct, source/product/version, native grain, time semantics, and relevant taxon/pathogen/method strata. Even then, categorical state comparison describes **evidence availability**, not relative surveillance intensity or adequate effort. CDC cumulative status, NEON sampled events, effort documentation, and testing availability are not interchangeable or poolable. `DRAG_CLOTH` and `FLAG_CLOTH` stay distinct. No cross-source normalization or ordinal mapping is approved.

M1 `OBSERVED_PATHOGEN_PREVALENCE` and M2 `EFFORT_NORMALIZED_COLLECTION_DENSITY` are **INFORMATIONAL ONLY** when shown beside this profile; neither is a coverage input, weight, or score. Current source-backed NEON M1 remains unavailable under its own contract for unresolved life stage. The v1 composite numeric coverage score is **DEFERRED**. So are county active-surveillance aggregation, temporal continuity threshold, minimum sample threshold, comparative source/method equivalence, and public prioritization/ranking. They require separate scientific/product review; #172 cannot derive ranks from this specification alone.

## Epic #159 consolidated eligibility clarification

The five approved remediation decisions are recorded in data ADR 0034 and
`surveillance-scientific-eligibility-v1`. Positive states require exact
publisher/product/version/vintage/construct eligibility and an approved
source-specific mapping for every required scientific field. Canonical IDs
are scientific keys; governed labels are display metadata. Unresolved
source-only county rows use the separate safe evidence envelope and never
establish county completeness. Testing-scope proof is explicit and carried to
#172; testing life stage remains optional. `PERIOD` collection evidence and
contradictory positive effort versus missingness fail closed without coercion.
The safe derived result implementing these rules is version 2; v1 history is
retained.

Independent expected cases are in `tests/fixtures/tick_surveillance/surveillance-coverage-v1-fixtures.json`; they are synthetic methodology expectations, not generated #171 output or source-backed execution proof.
